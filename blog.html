Technology September 23, 2025

Serbian Chamber of Commerce

We attended the event organized by the Serbian Chamber of Commerce, focused on Horizon Europe and other competitive programs, which brought together numerous representatives from companies, research organizations, and institutions. The event provided valuable insights into funding opportunities, international cooperation, and project development within European programs. Through discussions and networking sessions, we continued to strengthen business connections and build strategic partnerships that support long-term growth and innovation.

Cloud March 5, 2025

Together with DigitalDen, we participated in the Open Mic & BBQ event in the Netherlands, where we had the opportunity to present our project pitch deck to a diverse group of investors and industry experts. This participation allowed us to showcase our innovative solutions, exchange ideas with professionals from various sectors, and establish valuable connections for potential collaborations. Engaging in this international event strengthened our presence in the global innovation ecosystem and highlighted the impact of our technology-driven projects.

Mobile February 25, 2025

France-Western Balkans Business forum

The France–Western Balkans Business Forum brings together business leaders, institutions, and innovators from France and the Western Balkans to strengthen economic cooperation and foster new partnerships. The forum focuses on investment opportunities, innovation, and sustainable development across key industries. Through high-level discussions, networking sessions, and B2B meetings, participants connect, exchange expertise, and explore concrete opportunities for cross-border collaboration.

Security February 18, 2025

EXPO, Brisel

SYSQBIT attended the Odoo Conference, a premier gathering where industry leaders, innovators, and technology enthusiasts came together to explore the latest trends and developments in business management software. During the event, we engaged in in-depth discussions with experts and key stakeholders, gaining valuable insights into emerging practices, tools, and solutions within the ERP ecosystem. Through these interactions, we successfully established strategic partnerships with Odoo, enhancing our ability to deliver fully integrated, scalable, and efficient business solutions tailored to diverse client needs. This collaboration not only strengthens our capacity to provide advanced ERP systems but also enables us to implement innovative digital tools that optimize operations, improve workflow efficiency, and drive business growth.

DevOps February 10, 2025

Horizons of Innovation Forum

SYSQBIT participated in the Horizons of Innovation Forum 2025, an event that brought together researchers, innovators, and changemakers from across Europe and the Western Balkans. The forum provided a unique platform for exchanging knowledge, exploring cutting-edge ideas, and engaging in meaningful discussions on innovation and translating research into real-world impact. Through keynote sessions, expert panels, and targeted B2B and R2R meetings, SYSQBIT was able to identify and establish new opportunities for collaboration and strategic partnerships. The event also allowed us to showcase our advanced digital solutions and connect with leading institutions, startups, and research teams, further reinforcing our commitment to driving technological innovation.
